# Changelog

Everything worth knowing about each version of the four packages, which move together: `@locker-protocol/agent-wallet-hyperliquid-trader`, `@locker-protocol/agent-wallet-hyperliquid-trader-mcp`, `@locker-protocol/agent-wallet-hyperliquid-signer` and `@locker-protocol/agent-wallet-vault`. The SHA-256 of every published tarball is in [`RELEASES.md`](RELEASES.md).

## 2.4.3, 2026-10-16

The assistant gives its own opinion on a market, after reading it as the daily pilot does. Beside it, three corrections of words, and one of the console's chat: the help of `lpa policy` no longer names a loss of a day, the explanation of the daily loss is gone from what a wallet is said to show, the waiting line of Locker Vault and of a mobile wallet no longer says Enter opens the page where it opens nothing, and the second question of a conversation in the desktop application is answered live again.

### Changed

- **Asked about a market, the assistant investigates, then gives its own opinion.** It used to answer a question such as "long or short on BTC?" with the figures it read and "the decision is yours, nothing here is financial advice", without a probability or a choice of its own. It now reads the coin first, without asking, and answers with a trade plan: the side, the entry, the stop loss, the take profit, the risk/reward, its probability in percent and the horizon, then the facts that weigh most with their sources, the view of each horizon (short, medium and long term when none is said; minutes to hours for a scalp), at most one line saying it is its reading of the data and not a guarantee, and an offer to place the order or to run a strategy that fits. It never asks a question back before answering (the amount, the horizon, the risk tolerance), never refuses an opinion and never lectures. It still places, closes or cancels no order you did not ask for: an order it proposes goes through the quote and your confirmation. It recognises twenty-two kinds of question by their meaning, in any language, from a scalp on one coin to the best coin to short now, each with what to read, how to reason and the template of the answer; the same are in the public skill package, so an assistant that drives `lpa` from outside answers the same way.

### Added

- **`lpa perps analyze --symbol <S> [--horizons ...]`**, the assistant's tool `analyze_market`: one market as the daily pilot reads it, its very dossier (every horizon from 5 m to 1 w, the regime, the funding, the open interest, the highs and lows, the EMAs and the ATR, the flow, the leaders and the top accounts on it, the calendar), with the horizons asked laid apart, the book's best prices and largest resting orders, the headlines of the last day and the climate the pilot's reader wrote last while it is under a day old. The web's pages come through the copy the pilot keeps, read at most once an hour. It signs nothing and calls no model.
- **`lpa perps scan [--side long|short|both] [--limit <n>]`**, the assistant's tool `scan_markets`: the coins of the moment ranked, the daily pilot's universe filter on the venue, the largest by volume read on their regime and placed in the week's range, the strongest trends up and down with their figures, and the pilot's own screen of its measured universe when there is one.

### Fixed

- **`lpa policy` says what the policy holds.** Its two sentences named "the orders and the loss of a day", though no share of a loss stops anything since 2.4.0. They now read "the largest order, the leverage, the markets and the orders of a day".
- **No line explains a daily loss any more beside what a browser extension or a mobile wallet shows.** The explanation of `maxDailyLossPct` served the mandate signed before 2.4.0 alone, which `lpa` never asks a wallet to sign and refuses with `MANDATE_OUTDATED`.
- **Under `LPA_NO_BROWSER`, the waiting line of Locker Vault and of a mobile wallet says Esc alone.** Enter opens nothing there (the desktop application's window is their page), yet the line said "Enter opens the page, Esc cancels" and a page closed was to be opened again by Enter. The line now says "Esc cancels", and a page closed reads "Open it again at its address, or press Esc to cancel". The browser extension's line, whose Enter opens its page since 2.4.2, and every line on a screen without `LPA_NO_BROWSER`, are unchanged.
- **The second question of a conversation in the desktop application streams its answer again.** Since 2.1.0, from the second question of the same conversation on, the assistant's text, its reasoning, the tools it called and their results were sent to the first question's page, already closed: nothing showed while the assistant wrote, and each piece of its answer came back at the end as another notice from the assistant. Each question now streams on its own page and ends with its own text, and only the assistant's own notices (the steps run out, the day's ceiling, a request declined) are notices.

## 2.4.2, 2026-10-16

Two corrections: the signing page of a browser extension opens when you press Enter, even where `lpa` opens no browser by itself, and a guardian whose start fails is said as a start that failed on a machine under load.

### Fixed

- **Enter opens the extension's signing page under `LPA_NO_BROWSER` too.** `LPA_NO_BROWSER` keeps `lpa` from opening a browser of its own accord, and it used to make Enter on the waiting line do nothing as well, though the line said "Enter opens the page". When the account signs with a browser extension, that page can only live in your browser: Enter now opens it there, on an address good for one visit, never the page's own. Each Enter gives a new one and the one before stops working. With Locker Vault or a mobile wallet, `LPA_NO_BROWSER` still opens nothing; on a machine reached over SSH, nothing opens either way.
- **`lpa unlock` says "The guardian did not start: ..." when the guardian's start fails, on a slow machine too.** The guardian takes its address first and answers any request that waited on its start with the reason it failed, a moment before it ends: when `lpa unlock`'s own wait was that request, the command gave the guardian's bare refusal (`BAD_ARGUMENTS`) where `LOCKED` and "did not start" were due. The start now waits for that guardian to end and says its reason as before. A guardian that neither answers nor ends within the wait is stopped, so a start said to have failed never comes up afterwards with the agent key in memory.

## 2.4.1, 2026-10-15

A correction of the daily pilot: a position by steps is sized within the largest order your policy and the mandate allow, and weighed against them before its first step, where a ladder sized on the equity alone could see its first step refused.

### Fixed

- **A position by steps is sized within the largest order your policy and the mandate allow.** The daily pilot sized its ladder on the equity and the charter alone, so on an account of $10 000 at 3x the first step of a long on BTC was 0.01031 BTC, $833.88 at its fill, which a policy of $100 an order refused: the day ended `order-refused`. The base stake is now lowered, the steps keeping their proportions, until the largest step is at most the narrower of the policy's `maxOrderUsd` and, on the real account, the mandate's, with half a percent kept under it for a first step at market whose fill sits above the price it was sized at; a DCA by the value is held so that its later buy, up to twice a step, fits too. The ladder's line in the verdicts says it once: `scaled to your $100 per order: base stake $6.63 where the equity allows $277.75, first step $19.39, largest order $98.72 (...)`. When the bound leaves the first step under Hyperliquid's $10, the day names it (`your $30 per order is too small for this ladder of 5 steps: the first step would be $5.66, under Hyperliquid's $10 minimum`) and nothing is placed. The same in `lpa pilot deliberate`, which the assistant asks before proposing a ladder.
- **A day by steps is weighed against the limits before its first step**, as a grid and a range already were: new verdicts `leverage` (the market's, the policy's and the mandate's), `policy` and `mandate` (`the largest step is $98.72, within the policy's $100.00 an order`, the first step weighed at its quote), `orders` (every step of a ladder at its prices is posted the day it opens, the first alone of a DCA, against the narrowest orders of a day) and, on the real account, `notional` (what those orders engage against the mandate's notional of a day). A ladder they would refuse half-way is refused whole, nothing sent, with the figure that lets it open. A replay, and a measure over history, have no policy and size as before.
- **`lpa pilot strategy show` and `use` no longer say a ladder's steps above the order bound would be refused**: the finding says the day scales the ladder down to the bound, with its steps and its stake, and refuses only when the first step then falls under the venue's $10; the mandate's notional of a day is weighed on the ladder scaled, and a DCA's on its first step.

## 2.4.0, 2026-10-15

The stops in percent taken out: a position may sit far below its entry for weeks and the strategy waits, where a stop at minus 10 % of the day only strung losses together. No share of a loss refuses an order any more, the day's nor the one since the start, no fall of the equity stops the daily pilot, and the mandate signed on the phone loses its field: it holds fifteen fields under the version `2` of its domain, and a mandate signed before is refused by `MANDATE_OUTDATED` until it is signed again. What bounds the money is unchanged: the size of an order, the leverage, the markets, the orders and the notional of a day, the risk:reward, the expiry, the other bounds of this computer, the model's budget, the pause after losses in a row, and the stops each strategy sets for itself.

Beside it: the controls of an order (its validity, reduce-only, its margin), an order at a trigger kept and filled on the simulation account, several accounts on one computer, each a profile of its own, an account that signs with Locker Vault, a browser extension or a mobile wallet, the notices that reach your phone only once you turn them on, a pilot that opens nothing new in the last day of its key nor on a day its model does not answer, and the measure of each shipped strategy on its card.

### Added

- **`lpa notices`, `notices on`, `notices off` and `notices test`.** The notices of the service and the pilot reach your phone through your hyperkeel account only once you turn them on: `notices.json`, a switch of this computer, off by default and off when it does not read. Only the kind of a notice and at most one whole number leave, never an address, an amount, the key or its password; each post is in the register of `lpa doctor --egress`. `lpa notices test` sends a test notice and says whether it left (`sent`, `how`, `words`). `lpa status --json` says `noticesOn` (`onAt`) while they are on.
- **Nothing new within 24 hours of the agent's end.** On the real account, a day of the daily pilot ends `key-ending` after its guard, no model asked; a grid or a range is not set again; the continuous pilot's openings are refused by `AGENT_EXPIRED`, closing, cancelling and moving a stop still allowed. What is held is still followed. A notice, `pilot-key-ending`, asks for `lpa agent renew`, and once the key is renewed the schedule plays the day again by itself. `lpa status --json` and `lpa pilot status --json` give `keyEnding` (`validUntil`, `hoursLeft`) within that day.
- **A day the model does not answer.** When every deliberation of a day failed on the provider's call, the day ends `model-down`, nothing new opened, what is held still followed; its end file and `lpa pilot day` carry `modelDown` (`detail`, `played`), the outage is dated in `pilot/model-outage.json`, and `lpa status --json` and `lpa pilot status --json` give it as `modelOutage`; a notice, `model-down`, says it. A model that abstains or answers in a shape that does not read is not an outage.
- **The measure of each shipped strategy on its card.** `lpa pilot strategy show` and `list` give each strategy `lpa` ships the measure it was shipped with, `measure`: from several starting days thirty days apart, in simulation on Hyperliquid's past candles and funding, no model and no order, the worst, the median and the best of the return, the worst fall, the trades, the days and the holding of the same markets over each run, overall and market by market, with its date, its markets, its candles, its parameters and what it assumes of the entries; `show` says it in words, always with its bounds, and names the figures of the charter in use that differ from those measured (`measureEnvelope`). A strategy of yours carries none.
- **`lpa pilot model-down [--nothing | --code]`**: with `--code`, on such a day the code's rule decides in the model's place (the breakout of `decider: code`), through the same verdicts, on a strategy it fits (mode `stop`, `breakoutDays`, a trailing exit); on any other the day opens nothing. Nothing new is the default, and nothing is chosen for you. `--json` gives `fallback`, `setAt`, `codeFits`, `codeFitsWords`, `lastOutage`, `words`; `lpa pilot status --json` gives `modelDown`.
- **The controls of an order.** `lpa perps quote`, `perps check` and `perps open` take `--tif gtc|ioc|alo` with `--type limit`, `--reduce-only` and `--margin cross|isolated`, and the server for assistants takes them on `perps_quote` and `perps_open` as `tif`, `reduce_only` and `margin`. Each is judged before anything is signed: a validity on a market order or a trigger, cross on a market that takes isolated only, a margin other than the one of the position held there, a reduce-only order that would add or has nothing to reduce; a limit posted to the book only that would cross it (`alo-crosses`) and an immediate-or-cancel limit that would not fill (`ioc-no-fill`) are refused. The quote says `tif`, `reduceOnly` and `marginMode`, and that the leverage is set for the whole market. A reduce-only order is a close: the policy lets it through and it takes no place in the day's count.
- **A bound of this computer on the margin: `lpa policy set --margin-modes isolated|cross,isolated|any`**, absent until set, refuses an opening in cross margin as `POLICY_REJECTED`. The order types name a limit by its validity: `limit`, `limit-ioc`, `limit-alo`; a policy of `limit` written before allows the limit that rests, and neither of the new ones until they are named.
- **An order at a trigger on the simulation account.** `lpa perps open --type trigger --paper` keeps an entry trigger that waits for its price, judged by the policy when it is posted and again when it fills, and fills it at its trigger once the mark reaches it, at each pass of the service, of `lpa pilot follow` and of a reading of the simulation account's orders or positions. `lpa perps orders --paper` lists the orders waiting and what a pass filled (`fired`); `lpa perps cancel --paper` cancels one by its number, or all with `--all`; `lpa paper init --reset` starts over without them.
- **Three ways to sign for your account: `lpa init --with vault|browser|mobile`**, Locker Vault when it is left out, exactly as before; a browser extension, on a page of this computer; or a mobile wallet, over WalletConnect. With an extension or a mobile wallet the account is proven first, before any money, by one signature that moves nothing, checked here and sent nowhere, and an address that is a contract is refused. `lpa setup` asks how the account signs at its account's step, Locker Vault first. **`lpa signer show`, `lpa signer use vault|browser|mobile`** (the same address only) **and `lpa signer forget`**; `lpa doctor` gains the check `signer`; the questions before a signature, `lpa status`, `lpa wallet address` (its field `signer`) and the tools of the server for assistants name the way the account signs.
- **A copy chooses what becomes of its positions at its end: `lpa copy start --at-end keep|close`** (`at_end` on `copy_start`), `keep` by default as before; `close` closes them at market, reduce-only, when its days run out. `lpa copy status` says `atEnd`.
- **`lpa journal export --with-fills`** gives each real line the result its order realized on Hyperliquid (`realizedPnlUsd`, before fees), tied by the order alone; a line of an order filled at once keeps its `oid`.
- **Ending a range says what a close filled in part left**, as ending a grid now does: `lpa pilot end-range --close` and `end-grid --close` read the account again after the close and say `remaining` (the size still held, its side, `filledPct`).
- **`lpa pilot plans` says why a step of a position by steps does not rest**: each step of the real account gives `resting`, `refused` and `refusedBy` (policy, minimum, funds, guardian, venue).
- **Profiles: `lpa profile list | add | use | rename | remove`.** Several accounts on one computer, ten at most. One account is one profile, a whole folder of `lpa` with its key, its mandate, its limits, its pilot, its simulation account, its journal, its memory and its service: `~/.lpa` is the first one, `principal`, the others live in `~/.lpa/profiles/<name>/`, and `~/.lpa/profiles.json` says which there are, the console port of each and the current one. `profile add` copies the model's settings and key and the hyperkeel connection; `profile remove` is refused while anything is open (a position, a resting order, a copy, a plan of the daily pilot) and while the agent is approved, unless `--agent-stays`. `profile list --json` gives each profile's name, folder, port, account, mode, pilot and next day, service, key and the server line of an assistant.
- **`--profile <name>` on any command**, and `LPA_PROFILE`: the account the line runs for. `LPA_HOME`, set, is read first, as before; then the profile named, then the current one, then `~/.lpa`. `lpa status` and `lpa doctor` say the profile they read (`profile`), and `lpa doctor` of the principal profile a line for each other one (`profiles`).
- **One service per profile**: `com.lockerprotocol.lpa.<name>` and `lpa-<name>.service`, with the profile's folder and console port; the principal profile keeps the names of before. `lpa service status` gives the service of each profile (`profiles`).
- **`lpa setup --profile <name>`** takes the whole way on that profile, the account step offering the accounts of the vault's sync already read that no other profile holds; **`lpa init --from-sync`** takes an account of that sync without a scan. An account another profile of the computer holds is refused by `init` and `setup`.
- **A memory every account shares**, `memory-shared.db` beside the profiles: the pilot's lessons with nothing of an account in them, and the notes and rules written with **`lpa pilot notes --shared`** and **`lpa pilot rules --shared`**, each with the name of its profile. A line with an address, an amount, a size or a balance stays with its account. A deliberation reads the shared rules, then its account's, then the shared notes, then its account's, the account's winning where both say the same.
- **The server for assistants serves one profile**: `locker-mcp --profile <name>`, or `LPA_PROFILE`, its folder kept from its start.

### Removed

- **The daily loss limit.** `maxDailyLossPct` (10 % by default) refuses nothing, in the command, the guardian, a copy or the pilot, and is said nowhere: not in `lpa policy show`, not on the policy line of `lpa status`, not on the settings page of `lpa config`, not in the model's brief. `lpa status --json` loses `today.loss` and `today.lossError`; `lpa policy show --json` and `lpa policy set --json` lose `maxDailyLossPct` (policy and defaults). `day.json`, the base it was measured from, is no longer read nor written, and the guardian no longer asks Hyperliquid the equity of 00:00 UTC; `lpa reset` still removes an old one. A copy's day keeps its start and its count only.
- **The loss since the start.** `maxTotalLossPct` and its base `totalLossBaseUsd`, the bound of this computer added in 2.3.0, refuse nothing and are said nowhere (`lpa policy show`, the policy line of `lpa status`); `policy show --json` and `policy set --json` lose both fields.
- **The drawdown stop of the daily pilot.** The charter's `maxDrawdownPct` (15 %) is gone with the armed equity it was measured from (`pilot/armed.json`, no longer read nor written): a day never ends `stopped` for a fall any more. The pause after `lossStreak` losses in a row stays.
- **`maxDailyLossPct` from the mandate.** The EIP-712 struct `Mandate` holds fifteen fields, the others in their order, under the version `2` of its domain; `lpa mandate show` loses the row `Stop for the day after a loss of`.

### Changed

- **The service's warnings no longer leave this computer by default.** They used to be posted to hyperkeel as soon as this computer was connected; they are now said in the console and the record only, until `lpa notices on`.
- **A mandate signed before 2.4.0 is refused whole: `MANDATE_OUTDATED`** (reason `outdated`), with `This mandate was signed before lpa 2.4.0, in a form this version no longer reads...` and the hint to sign it again. Every opening of the guardian under it is refused by that code, a live day of the pilot too; closing stays allowed. `lpa status` and `lpa mandate show` give the state `outdated`.
- **The old words still accepted, without effect.** `lpa policy set --max-daily-loss-pct <n>`, `--max-total-loss-pct <n>`, `--total-loss-base-usd <n>` and `lpa pilot charter --set maxDrawdownPct=<n>` change nothing, say so in one line, and carry `retired` in their JSON; the flags are no longer in the usage nor offered by the session. `lpa pilot rearm` is kept, no longer listed, and answers that there is nothing to arm again (`rearmed: false`).
- **Files written before read as they are.** A `policy.json` with `maxDailyLossPct`, `maxTotalLossPct` or `totalLossBaseUsd`, a `charter.json` with `maxDrawdownPct` and a copy with its day's starting equity read without error, the field ignored and left out at the next write.
- `lpa uninstall` stops and removes the service and the guardian of every profile; `--all` removes every profile with the folder of the principal one.

### Fixed

- `lpa reset` removes the switch of the server for assistants (`mcp.json`) with the rest, so the next start has the server on and remembers no assistant taken out.

## 2.3.0, 2026-10-14

What the desktop window Hyper Agentic Trader asks of the program, to keep the money safe and to show everything to the person who wants to see it: the day's loss said and measured from one base, bounds of the money in this computer's limit, a key that locks itself, the gestures that end everything at once and say first what they give back; the account read as Hyperliquid keeps it (its executions, its funding, its rates, its movements of money, its margin) and a journal that exports; the days of the pilot listed with what they cost; and a switch of its own for the server for assistants, for hyperkeel and for each plugin.

### Added

- **`lpa status` says the day's loss.** The line of the day gives what is lost since the base, the limit in dollars and in percent, and the base; `--json` gives `today.loss` (`usd`, `pnlUsd`, `baseUsd`, `baseFrom`, `equityUsd`, `limitPct`, `limitUsd`, `limitFrom`, `reached`, `book`), computed as the policy computes it when it refuses, on the book of the mode in use, the limit narrowed by a mandate in force.
- **Seven bounds of this computer's limit**, each optional and absent until set: the notional held across every market (`--max-open-notional-usd`), on one market (`--max-market-notional-usd`) and as a share of the equity (`--max-market-exposure-pct`), the order types (`--order-types`), the distance from the mid of the price an order signs (`--max-price-deviation-bps`), the orders in sixty minutes (`--max-orders-per-hour`), and the loss since the start (`--max-total-loss-pct`, `--total-loss-base-usd`). An order that lowers what a bound weighs always goes. They do not enter the mandate signed on the phone.
- **`lpa unlock --idle-minutes <n>`**: the key locks itself once nothing has been signed for that long; `lpa status` says when.
- **`lpa perps cancel --all`** cancels every resting order, of every funded dex or of one market (`--symbol`), after one go, one row each; the stops and targets of open positions stay unless `--with-stops`; `--dry-run` lists them.
- **`lpa perps close --all` says what each close gives back**, dry run or not: each row its estimated result after its fees (`resultUsd`) and its margin (`marginUsd`), and `totals` the lot's result, closing fees, margin and what comes back on the account (`backUsd`), a sum null as soon as one row lacks its figure.
- **A close at the mid, then at market: `lpa perps close --symbol <S> --at mid [--wait <s>]`** rests a reduce-only limit at the mid for the size asked, then cancels it after the wait and closes what is left at market, so the close always ends closed; `--json` says what filled at the mid and what went at market (`atMid`).
- **Ending a grid or a range is priced before the gesture.** `lpa pilot end-grid --preview` and `end-range --preview` say the plan's balance since it was armed (`sinceArmed`: round trips or trades, fees, funding, the model, net) and, when something is held, the close at market quoted with its fees and the balance once closed (`closeEstimate`); nothing is done.
- **A refusal says its cause and its figures as fields.** With `--json`, and from the console and the live page, the refusals a window can meet carry `reason`, a stable word (`no-key`, `guardian-not-running`, `wrong-password`, `needs-go`, `arbitrum-usdc-short`, `below-minimum`, `no-model` and others), and `facts`, the figures of the sentence by name, beside the code, the sentence and the hint, which are unchanged.
- **A deliberation under the assistant's ceiling of the day: `SPEND_CEILING`.** `lpa pilot deliberate` is refused before any call once the day's spend on the model has reached the ceiling set for the assistant in `lpa config`, under a new code, `SPEND_CEILING`, with its cause (`day-ceiling`) and its figures (`spentUsd`, `ceilingUsd`).
- **A size in dollars: `--usd <dollars>`** in place of `--size` for `lpa perps quote`, `perps check` and `perps open`. `lpa` turns the dollars into the token at the price it quotes (the limit, the trigger, the mid of a market order or its estimated fill) and rounds the size down to the market's grid, so the notional quoted is never above the dollars asked; `--json` says it under `fromUsd` (`usd`, `px`), and the order goes out at the size the quote said.
- **`lpa perps deposit --preview`** says the USDC and the ETH the account holds on Arbitrum One and what the deposit's gas is expected to cost and can cost at most, priced on the node now; nothing is signed.
- **The notes of a quote have codes**: `noteCodes`, in the order of `notes` (`isolated-only`, `limit-crosses`, `entry-trigger`, `partial-fill`, `below-minimum`, `other`).
- **`lpa perps fills`** gives the executions of the account over a period (`--since`, `--until`, `--symbol`, `--limit`), read from Hyperliquid: the price, the size, the value, the fees in one total, whether it took liquidity, the result realized, the order, the execution and its hash, with the totals of the period; on the simulation account, its orders, closes and liquidations from the journal, each with what it realized.
- **`lpa perps funding`** gives the funding the positions paid and received over a period, hour by hour, positive when paid, by market and in total, read from Hyperliquid; on the simulation account, its total since it opened.
- **`lpa perps fees`** gives the rates the account pays, taking from the book and resting on it, each one total with the commission when the account authorized it, Hyperliquid's tier the account is on and its volume of the last days.
- **`lpa perps ledger`** gives the movements of money of the account over a period, read from Hyperliquid: deposits, withdrawals, sends and transfers between dexes, each with its way, its amount, its fee and its hash, and what came in and went out.
- **The margin as Hyperliquid keeps it.** Each position of `lpa perps positions` says `marginMode` (`cross` or `isolated`); each dex of `lpa perps balance` says the margin its positions hold, the maintenance margin of its cross positions, their notional and the share of the cross value the maintenance takes (`marginUsedUsd`, `maintenanceMarginUsd`, `notionalUsd`, `maintenanceRatioPct`); each order of `lpa perps orders` says the size it was placed with (`origSz`). They are read from the answers the rows already came from.
- **`lpa perps positions` gives the funding since each position opened**: `fundingSinceOpen` (Hyperliquid's own figure, positive when paid) and `pnlWithFundingUsd`, the latent result with it, on the real account.
- **The journal for the expert.** A line of an order keeps the mid it was decided at (`quotedMidPx`), its order on Hyperliquid when the answer gives it back (`oid`) and what executed at once (`avgPx`, `filledSz`); `lpa journal` reads a period (`--until`), one market (`--symbol`) and some kinds (`--kind`); **`lpa journal export --out <file>`** writes every line of a period, past the thousand a reading gives, as CSV or JSON lines (`--as`), the fees one total, mode 600; `lpa perps fills` gives each execution the mid its order was decided at when the journal keeps that order.
- **The days of the pilot listed: `lpa pilot day --list [--days <n>]`**, one row a day with its strategy, how it was chosen and what that call cost, its outcome, its result and fees once closed, the model's dollars and its equity, a day of the real account with the equity Hyperliquid keeps for its end. The choice of a day keeps its cost and the regimes it read; the end of a day keeps its book and the equity it read.
- **`lpa pilot day --dossier`** gives the dossier of a day as it was sent, its whole text, with its sections, its fingerprint and whether the text still hashes to it, who read it (`sentTo`: the model, the code alone, nobody yet) and the brain's block sent with it.
- **The end of a day and the marks of the daily clock carry their figures**: `lpa pilot run-now` gives back the figure that ended the day (`figure`), a day refused with its first failing verdict's; the clock keeps it for the day played, and the code, cause and figures of a day or a follow that failed.
- **`lpa pilot deliberate --estimate`** says what a deliberation on one market costs before it is asked: the average, lowest and highest of the last ten made on this computer with the model in use, read from the register of the calls, and the day's spend against the ceiling (`today`); the model is not asked and nothing is written.

## 2.2.0, 2026-10-12

The daily pilot no longer holds one way of trading. Its charter keeps how much (the risk, the leverage, the budgets) and names a strategy: how the pilot trades, under a name and in one sentence, in a JSON file that is read and never executed. Eighteen strategies are shipped, a person can make their own from one of them, measure it over two years without a model, put it in use, let the pilot choose one each day, or have the model draft one from a sentence; before any of it, `lpa` says what the policy and the mandate in force would refuse.

### Added

- **`lpa pilot strategy`: list, show, use, save, remove, choice, draft.** `list` gives the shipped strategies and yours, each in one sentence with its kind and whether it is the measured charter or the one in use. `show` reads one by its name or from a file and changes nothing: what the trader is told of it, its figures under the charter in use, the smallest account it opens anything on, and what the policy and the mandate would refuse of it at an equity. `use` puts one in use, `--preview` saying the effect first; `save` writes your own, from a shipped one with `--set "<field>=<value> ..."` or from a file, every figure read by the charter's rules and its bounds; `remove` removes one of yours, never the one in use.
- **Four kinds of method.** By steps (`ladder`: the measured charter, both ways, out in parts, and two DCAs, by the time or by the value), with a stop (`stop`: a target, a stop that trails, or a part first; a breakout, a pullback to the trend, an excess of the RSI, a fade of the funding), a grid (`grid`: long, short or neutral across the week's range, what to do out of it, and whether it starts again), and a range (`range`: two entries at the borders of a range, the first filled cancelling the other, out at a share of its width or at a stop beyond the border). A plan keeps the method that opened it: changing strategy never touches a position held.
- **The eighteen shipped strategies**: `ladder-long` (the default, the charter measured over two years), `ladder-both`, `ladder-tiers`, `dca-time`, `dca-value`, `swing-stop`, `swing-trail`, `swing-partial`, `breakout`, `trend-pullback`, `donchian`, `rsi-excess`, `funding-fade`, `grid-long`, `grid-short`, `grid-neutral`, `range-reversal` and `band-revert`.
- **A strategy decided by the code alone.** `decider: "code"` arms a breakout with no model asked: `donchian` rests each day a trigger past the high of the last thirty days over the daily EMA 50, or past their low under it, its stop two daily ATRs away, then trailing. Such a day costs nothing in tokens, and `lpa pilot schedule` and `lpa pilot charter` say so.
- **The choice of the day.** A charter may name two to five strategies, and each day, before the day, the pilot chooses the one it plays: by a table of the leading market's regime, or by the model in one brief call whose answer can only be one of the names allowed. The day then runs whole under the strategy chosen. A regime the table does not name, a model that does not answer or a name outside the list play the strategy in use, and the day says so. `lpa pilot strategy choice --today` says what today would choose, and writes nothing.
- **A strategy drafted from one sentence.** `lpa pilot strategy draft --message "<what you want>"`: the model starts from the strategy nearest to it and changes the figures asked; the code reads every figure by the charter's rules, sends a refusal back for a correction, says the strategy as `show` does, and writes it only when the person confirms it in their terminal. `--preview` writes nothing, and its answer gives the figures changed as `save --set` takes them, so a program that drives `lpa` writes the same strategy on the person's word. `save` says the strategy as `show` does, at an equity, before anything is written; `lpa pilot day` says how the day's strategy was chosen.
- **Signal rules the code judges before any order**: `breakoutDays`, `rsiExcess`, `fundingFadePct` (the funding of the last seven days), and `followLeaders` (a long only where the top accounts of the leaderboard are net long on the market, a short only where they are net short).
- **`lpa pilot calibrate --strategy <name>`** measures any strategy over two years of candles, without a model: a ladder as it plays, a stop, a grid or a range by its own measure, each with its return, its worst fall and its fees, from one or several starting days (`--starts`). `lpa pilot replay --strategy <name>` plays past days under it.
- **`lpa pilot end-grid` and `lpa pilot end-range`** end a grid or a range the pilot holds, now: its resting orders cancelled and its plan removed, what it holds closed at market (`--close`) or left on the account as yours (`--keep`); `--preview` does nothing.

### Changed

- `lpa pilot charter` reads the charter whole with the strategy in use; `--set` on a figure of the strategy keeps it in a strategy of your own, never in a shipped one. A charter of the version before reads as it did, and the object `charter` of its answer keeps its shape.
- A trigger order of the simulation account is weighed by the policy when it is placed, as a real one is.

### Fixed

- **A gesture on the service from another folder reached the service of the first.** The service has one name on a machine, and `lpa service stop`, `restart`, `start` and `uninstall` acted on it whatever folder asked: run with another `LPA_HOME`, `uninstall` removed the service that runs `lpa` for the first folder. They now refuse, naming the folder that service serves and the command to run for it; `lpa uninstall` and `lpa reset` of another folder leave that service as it is.
- **A live day of the pilot needs a mandate that allows the pilot.** `lpa pilot run-now --live` and `lpa pilot schedule --on --live` asked a mandate that allows `trade` only, the use of your own orders; they now ask `trade` and `pilot`, what the phone warns you about. While no such mandate is in force, what the real account holds is still followed (its exits, its stops, a cancel, a close), and nothing new is opened. A day in simulation that follows a plan of the real account says first that it places real orders for it.
- **A stop the venue refuses with the price already past it closes the position.** An entry by trigger goes alone and its stop is posted once it fired; with the price past the stop by then, the venue refuses it, and the position ran without one. It is now closed at the market, as the stop would have; a range does the same.

It now waits while the entry rests, and drops the plan without a balance once it is neither resting nor held.

- **What the guardian opens is quoted on the account's own fee tier**, as the commands quote it, rather than at the base rate.
- **`lpa service uninstall` asks, as `lpa service stop` does.** It stopped and removed the service with nobody at the terminal and no `--yes`; it now needs your yes or `--yes`. `lpa service logs` from a folder the service does not serve is refused by the folder it serves, and `lpa doctor` names that folder rather than offering to install a service there.

- **A strategy file is read within its bound** (`lpa paper replay --strategy`): a pipe or a device at its name is refused by its kind at once, where the command could wait for ever.
- **Two files of the folder that do not read are said as such.** An `assistant-keys.json` of another version was reported as Hyperliquid's words, and a `policy.json` that does not read as a limit that held; both are now `LOCAL_STATE`.
- On Windows, `lpa uninstall` refuses a path its command line would read otherwise (`%` or `"`) before anything is touched, and removes the folder a settings link points at.
- The help of `lpa paper init`, `lpa live` and `lpa perps check` says every flag they take; `lpa live --json` gives the page's address without its key; the console's chat answers with the headers its pages send.
- **A strategy with a stop or a range has no smallest account of its own.** `lpa pilot charter`, `lpa pilot schedule` and `lpa setup` gave them the ladder's, $120 with the shipped charter, and `lpa pilot schedule --on --live` refused for a deposit an account that sizes its orders above Hyperliquid's $10 floor. They now say the strategy sizes each order from its stop, and an order under $10 is refused when sized.
- **The strategy `donchian` is said as the code plays it.** `lpa pilot strategy show` presented it with a trader's words (a target named, the model's cost, a decision to take) while no model is asked; it says the code's rule, its trigger, its stop and its aim, under "Decided by the code, no model asked".
- **A rule of a signal is refused where nothing reads it.** `breakoutDays`, `rsiExcess` and `fundingFadePct` under a grid or a range, and `pullbackAtr` under any kind but a strategy with a stop, were taken and never judged; they are refused by name. An entry zone under 100 written for any kind but a ladder is refused the same way, and a ladder turned into another kind takes the zone of that kind, 100.
- **The measure of a grid reads the range the day reads.** `lpa pilot calibrate --strategy` read a grid's range over the strategy's hours as given, where the day reads the dossier's nearest horizon (a day, seven days or thirty): a grid of 336 hours was measured on fourteen days and played on seven. The line now names the horizon, and says that neither the regime nor the events are judged.
- **The pairs of `--set` are read together.** `lpa pilot strategy save --set` read the strategy after each pair, so two figures bound together (a minimum under its maximum, the code's decider with its days and its exit) had to come in one order; they are now applied together and read once. `lpa pilot charter --set` refuses a second pair by name instead of reading it into the first one's value.
- **A DCA whose first limit was never reached buys nothing.** In simulation, in the replay and in the brain's judgement, its first step was bought at market at its first moment, above the price the trader named; it now waits for its price, as on the real account, and its later steps fall due at their moments from the decision.
- **`lpa pilot strategy choice --today` reads the markets the day reads.** Under the universe it read the regime of a market named `*` and always answered the strategy in use; it reads the candidates the screen puts on the table (the default markets when the measure is stale), and names them in `on`.
- **The cost-share of a DCA says what its later steps pay.** The verdict weighed the first step alone, at the maker's rate, while a DCA buys its later steps at market whatever the price; it now says their fees there and back and their share of the target, which the exit absorbs.
- **A target counts through the charter's margin in the measure of a strategy with a stop.** `throughBps` reached the measures of a ladder, a grid and a range only; a stop's target now counts once the price went that far past it, its stop at the touch, and the line says the margin.
- **A draft is told that `followLeaders` is true or false.** The model was told it was "a figure", answered 1, and lost one of its three turns.

- **Simulation mode refuses a live day of the daily pilot.** `lpa pilot run-now --live` and `lpa pilot schedule --on --live` went ahead while simulation mode was on, and a schedule on the real account kept playing real days; a live day is now refused by name until `lpa paper off`.

## 2.1.0, 2026-10-08

Not published on its own: these changes reached the registry within 2.2.0.

What a program that drives `lpa` needs in order to show a person what the terminal shows them: the same figures, read from the answer instead of being pulled back out of its sentences, the daily pilot's charter and its day readable and changeable by name, an order judged without being placed, and the session's assistant reachable over the console. One word changes everywhere a person reads it: the practice account is the simulation account.

### Added

- **The session's assistant answers over the console's API.** `POST /api/chat` takes a question of 1 to 2000 characters and sends the turn back as it happens, event by event: the model's reasoning (`think`), its text (`say`), each command it calls (`tool`) and what the command answered (`result`), the question of an order (`ask-go`) and the end of the turn (`done`, with the steps taken, the day's spend and the name of the conversation). A turn that stops on a refusal ends on `refused`, with the code and the way out. One conversation at a time: a second question while a turn runs is answered 409 and never queued. Where the terminal asks in a menu, the route asks in the page and waits for at most 120 seconds, the life of a quote: `POST /api/chat/go` carries the yes or the no and, for a yes, the console password, checked on a brake of its own so a refusal elsewhere never holds a conversation back. The routes are offered only by the console of the service, only to a signed-in session, and only from the console's own origin. - **`lpa perps check`: an order judged, and not acted on.** It quotes the order and says whether the limits let it through at this moment: `passes: true`, or the refusal `lpa perps open` would give with its code, its sentence, its hint and what the limit teaches. - `lpa perps close` carries `resultUsd`, the estimated result of the exit, signed and to the cent: the figure the terminal already printed.

- **`lpa pilot day` gives the day in parts beside its story:** the end with the figure that stopped it and the bound it was held to, the guard's count of positions, orders and stale orders cancelled, the dossier with its sections, what went unread, its size and its fingerprint, the table of the day with each market's place in its week, the reader's climate, each deliberation with its decision and its market, the vote, the verdicts with their figures, the order and the balance, and the names of the day's files with their folder. The story the terminal prints is read from the same parts.

## 2.0.0, 2026-10-05

First version.

### Safety guarantees of this version

- The mandate has a durable state: `~/.lpa/mandate-state.json` keeps the highest nonce signed here and the revocation, so an older mandate put back, a revoked one put back or a file gone opens nothing, in this guardian and the next; a renewed agent needs a mandate of its own; `lpa mandate revoke` lasts; `lpa mandate release` (the person's password) goes back to the local policy alone; `lpa unlock` and `lpa status` say the mandate's standing.
- The guardian counts what signs in one breath: N openings at once cannot each pass the day's ceilings, N live copies at once cannot each take the whole copy budget; a place taken for an order that was not placed is given back; an equity that does not read opens nothing; a clock that went back keeps the day's counters; an order placed with a journal that could not be written comes back placed, with the reason.
- Live copies: the execution is written as taken before the order leaves (never placed twice after a crash), a reduction that could not be placed is kept and tried again, the budget stays counted while the positions are open and at the read back only a mandate in force takes a copy up, the person's own account is never copied.
- Plugins: a manifest with a control or a bidirectional character is refused, the consent screen is plain text, the MCP server lists a plugin's tools from its files under a free word only.
- MCP: `QUOTE_STALE` when the book (paper or real), the account, the market, the side, the size or the leverage changed between the quote and the go; an order cancelled while the guardian signs it is answered all the same.
- A relayed page is opened in the browser on its one-visit code, never on the console's address that carries the token; the token and the journal are never written through a link; a market named ALL is a market in the session.
- The home: a file holding `null` is not a file that is absent (a policy replaced by four bytes no longer goes back to the defaults); `LPA_HOME` refuses control characters and is judged on what it really names (a link included); a folder is a home by its `policy.json`, its paper account or a sealed key, never by a `config.json` alone; the journal is read within a bound of 8 MB from its end; what a disk answers is named local with its errno.
- The SDK: the 10 s timer of `/info` covers the body; the candles share a memo entry within its tick and the memo is bounded; a user-signed message carrying a field its types do not declare is refused; the UR sequence bound reads every spelling of a length.
- Every published launch line and configuration runs the MCP server with `npx -y --ignore-scripts`; the installer sets `umask 077`.

### More guarantees of this version

- `lpa mandate sign` reads Hyperliquid's clock and refuses to sign more than 5 minutes away from it. `mandate sign` and `mandate show` name the account and the instant signed, and Locker Vault shows both on its card. `lpa mandate revoke` says which of its three outcomes happened.
- A vault answer signed by another account than the one asked is refused with a code of its own, `VAULT_ANSWER_MISMATCH`.
- Copies: a copy that ended is kept beside a new copy of the same trader, and `copy status --all` shows both; `lpa copy resume` asks a running guardian to take its paused copies up; a file of `copies/` that is not a copy of lpa is named by `copy status` and fails `lpa doctor`; `lpa paper on` warns when a live copy runs.
- MCP: every string of a tool has a maximum length in its schema; sizes and prices are plain decimals; a batch holds 64 messages at most, and one failing message no longer takes the batch with it; a line too long is refused as it arrives; a quote is held, not spent, while the world is read again, and the account is part of what must not move between the quote and the go.
- Plugins: a plugin runs on a frozen copy of its approved files; its approval is read again before each call it makes; a plugin that takes a word already taken says so first; an archive over 32 MiB, or 64 MiB unpacked, is refused; the consent screen says that a plugin knows the user name, the name of the computer and its network addresses.
- Policy and quote: a take profit or a stop loss must be on its side of the entry; the exits shown and journalled are the ones the order signs; one risk:reward computation serves the quote and the policy; `lpa policy set` accepts only what a mandate can sign.
- Files: a journal line cut short is closed before the next one; settings files are read within 4 MB; `lpa reset` removes the service first, makes sure no guardian answers before it erases anything, and names the live copies that still hold positions.
- Paper tape and replay: a tape line that does not read is skipped and counted; the tape is written aside, then moved into place; a replay window that holds no candle is refused with what the tape covers.
- The SDK: each `/info` try has its own 10 s window; a market typed in another case is found under its exact name; a number that is not finite, or would be written with an exponent, is refused before it is signed; a sale below the smallest price rises to it instead of 0; a feed whose socket goes silent is closed and resumes.
- The vault library: a sync QR is bounded in characters and in bytes and walked before the library reads it; a signature answer is bounded, its r and s checked on the curve; two animated QR in sight are said as such; a frame count no reader takes is refused before anything is drawn.
- The installer: every path a launcher names is quoted, so a home holding a quote or a dollar sign installs launchers that run; a new launcher is run before it replaces the one that worked; a node reached through a `.` or an empty entry of the PATH is refused; an archive with no SHA-256 written into the installer is refused; `lpa uninstall --all` removes the folder the installer made even when it holds only the settings.

### The command, `@locker-protocol/agent-wallet-hyperliquid-trader`

- `lpa init`: set up with Locker Vault through the QR ceremony. A local page on this computer reads the vault's accounts through the webcam, then shows the approval of a new agent key for the phone to sign. The agent key is created and sealed here, under a password typed in the terminal, before the phone signs. `--days` sets how long the agent lives, 7 by default.
- `lpa unlock` and `lpa lock`: start the guardian, a background process that holds the agent key in memory and signs orders, and take the key back out of memory. The password is typed by the person, never read from the environment or from an argument.
- `lpa doctor` and `lpa status`: the setup check by check, and the account, agent, guardian and mode at a glance. Neither signs anything.
- `lpa wallet address` and `lpa wallet balances`: the vault account, its ETH and USDC on Arbitrum, its margin and spot on Hyperliquid.
- Reads: `lpa perps venues`, `markets`, `quote`, `positions`, `balance`, `orders`, with `--dex <name>` and `--all-dexes` for the HIP-3 dexes. Markets are sorted by 24 hour volume and halted ones are marked.
- `lpa perps regime --symbol <S>` and `lpa perps ranges`: a market's trend, structure, momentum, RSI, volatility and volume; the markets trading in a range now, with their width, support and resistance.
- Orders: `lpa perps open`, `close`, `cancel`, `modify`. Each one quotes, checks the local policy, asks the person, then the guardian rebuilds the order, checks the policy again and signs. `--dry-run` stops before any signature; `--yes` is the person's, never the agent's; `close --all` closes every position. Closing and cancelling are always allowed by the policy.
- Movements of funds: `lpa perps deposit`, `withdraw`, `transfer`, and `lpa agent revoke`. All four are signed by Locker Vault on the phone, never by the agent key.
- Paper account: `lpa paper init --budget`, `status`, `on`, `off`. Fills on Hyperliquid's real book, counts the same fees, needs no key and no vault, and works before `lpa init`. With paper mode on, every `perps` read comes from the paper account, and `modify`, `cancel`, `deposit`, `withdraw` and `transfer` are refused so nothing reaches the real account by mistake.
- `lpa paper record` and `lpa paper replay --strategy <file.json>`: record the market into a local tape (candles at 1 m, 5 m, 1 h and 4 h, and funding), then replay a strategy written as a JSON file on it, with the win rate, its 95 % lower bound, the costs and a verdict.
- `lpa policy show` and `lpa policy set`: the limits every order is checked against, by the command and again by the guardian. Defaults are 100 USD per order, leverage 3, the main dex only, 20 orders a day, a daily loss of 10 % of equity, and a risk:reward guard at 3. Widening a limit takes effect at the next `lpa unlock`.
- `lpa journal [--since 24h]`: what was done, what was refused and what was paid, with the fees of the period as one total.
- `lpa config`: a local settings page for the assistant, the limits, paper trading, the account and the hyperkeel connection.
- `lpa copy start`, `stop`, `status`, `resume`: copy a trader on paper, in the background, inside the guardian, so it keeps running when the session is closed. Nothing is signed. The parts of one order that crosses the book are copied as a single order. Four ways to size an opening with `--sizing`: `proportional` to the equities and never more than `--max-scale` times the trader's size (1 by default), `mirror`, `percent` of the copy's equity as margin, or a `fixed` margin. When the live feed drops or the computer restarts, the trader's executions are read back from Hyperliquid before the copy goes on: a reduction is mirrored however late, an opening more than two minutes old is not chased.
- `lpa copy start --live`: copy a trader on the real account, signed by the agent key in the guardian, under the policy and a mandate signed on the phone that names the trader, with room in its copy budget. The guardian must hold the key; paper stays the default. Each order counts in the day's orders and notional; stopping the copy leaves its positions open, and says so.
- `lpa mandate sign`, `show`, `revoke`: the agent's limits signed once on the phone (Locker Vault shows every bound on its own card), checked by the guardian before every opening, never wider than the local policy. It adds a ceiling on the notional opened per day, the fee recipient and its rate, and what the key may be used for (orders from the agent, live copies of named traders).
- `lpa plugins install`, `list`, `inspect`, `remove`, and `lpa <plugin> <command>`: plugins that add commands and MCP tools. A plugin runs in a separate Node process under the permission model, reading its own folder only, and reaches the wallet only through the capabilities it declared and the person approved (markets, account, journal, paper trading, order proposals); none of them signs for the real account. No script of a plugin runs at install; its files are fingerprinted, and a changed plugin does not run.
- The guardian answers only requests that carry the token it writes at start, which a plugin cannot read.
- `lpa hyperkeel login`, `status`, `logout`, `brief`, `leaders`, `follows`, `follow`, `unfollow`, `alerts`: market data and alerts from hyperkeel.com. Optional, and only reached once you connect.
- `lpa service install`, `status`, `logs`, `start`, `stop`, `restart`, `uninstall`, plus `lpa console` and `lpa console password`: run the guardian around the clock as a service of the system, on this computer or on a server, with a console on `127.0.0.1:7717` behind its own password. The console unlocks the service, renews the agent through the phone, shows the pages of commands typed on the server and lists its warnings.
- `lpa reset` and `lpa uninstall [--all]`: wipe the local state, or remove the command from this computer.
- `lpa` alone opens an interactive session: the same commands as `/` lines with Tab completion over the whole grammar, live views for the markets, a chart with its order book and trades, the hyperkeel radar, the leaderboard and any trader's page, and an assistant on the model of your choice. Seven providers are supported, local models included; without one configured, nothing leaves the machine for an assistant.
- Every command answers in text, or in JSON with `--json` or `--format json`. A refusal carries a stable code and what to do next.
- Fees are shown as one total everywhere they appear: the quote, the confirmation, the paper account, the journal and the JSON output.
- One-line installers for macOS, Linux and Windows, which fetch Node when it is missing, check what they download by SHA-256, need no administrator rights and put everything in `~/.lpa`.

### The MCP server, `@locker-protocol/agent-wallet-hyperliquid-trader-mcp`

- 42 tools over stdio, plus the tools of the plugins the user installs (their answers marked untrusted), each one running the `lpa` command of the same name through the same library, so the local policy, the paper mode and the refusals are exactly the command's.
- Reads, orders, movements of funds, the paper account, the policy, the journal, the doctor, hyperkeel and the paper copies. Orders need `confirm: true`; without it, `perps_open` and `perps_close` answer with the quote and sign nothing.
- Movements of funds answer with the exact line for the user to type, because only the phone signs them.
- `hyperkeel_brief` and `hyperkeel_leaders` read hyperkeel's routes for agents, which need no account.
- Every tool carries its four annotations, so a client can tell a read from an order before it calls.
- `mandate_show`, `mandate_sign` (answers the line the user types; the phone signs) and `mandate_revoke` (only restricts). `copy_start` takes `live`, `sizing`, `max_scale`, `percent` and `fixed_margin`.
